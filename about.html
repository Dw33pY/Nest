Our Journey

The Evolution of Nest

2018 — The Inception

Founded in a small apartment in Berlin with a laptop and a vision to elevate short-term rentals from forgettable to unforgettable.

2020 — Global Expansion

Expanded to 30 countries, partnering with world-renowned architects to list properties that redefine what a rental can be.

2022 — The Concierge Launch

Introduced our white-glove concierge service, offering personalized itineraries, private chefs, and bespoke experiences for every guest.

Today — Industry Leader

Recognized as the premier platform for design-led travel, setting the standard for quality, aesthetics, and heartfelt hospitality worldwide.
